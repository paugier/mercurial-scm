# About the Mercurial Wiki archive

The wiki's use to the project has been replaced by a mix of static pages on this website
([feel free to contribute!](https://foss.heptapod.net/mercurial/hg-website)) and issues
on [Heptapod](https://foss.heptapod.net/mercurial/mercurial-devel/-/work_items/10001).

A static archive of most of the (non-spam) pages from the former wiki is kept at
[the original URL](https://wiki.mercurial-scm.org).

Certain important pages have been or will be redirected from the archive to their new
version on this website. **If anything important is missing and should be migrated to
either of those places, please reach out.**

You can read the announcement of the wiki's demise in our
[mailing list](https://lists.mercurial-scm.org/pipermail/mercurial/2025-July/106680.html).
