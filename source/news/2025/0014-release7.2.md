# Mercurial 7.2 released

This new major version has been delayed for a good while. We are reworking so much of the
internals these days that it felt prudent to delay the final release as much as needed
and roll out nightly versions to (consenting) users until we felt comfortable enough
about the stability of the release.

See the [release notes](../../relnotes/7.2) for details.
